Subsidies provided by Government

Subsidy schemes help farmers reduce the cost of agricultural inputs like fertilizers, irrigation, and machinery.
These schemes encourage modern farming practices and improve productivity.
Government subsidies make farming affordable and profitable for small and marginal farmers.

Fertilizer Subsidy

Subsidy Scheme

Provides fertilizers at affordable prices to support crop production.

PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana

Subsidy Scheme

Supports irrigation facilities like drip and sprinkler systems.

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi

Subsidy Scheme

Provides direct income support of ₹6000 per year to farmers.

Solar Pump Subsidy

Subsidy Scheme

Encourages use of solar energy by subsidizing solar-powered pumps.

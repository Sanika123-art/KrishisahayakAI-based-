Insurance provide by Government

Insurance schemes for farmers provide financial protection against crop loss, natural disasters, accidents, and life risks.
These schemes help farmers recover from unexpected losses caused by floods, droughts, pests, or other calamities by offering compensation at low premium rates.
Government-backed insurance ensures income stability and security for farmers and their families.

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

Insurance Scheme

Protect crops from natural disasters and losses.

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana

Insurance Schemes

Offers life insurance cover of ₹2 lakh to farmers in case of death.

Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

Insurance Schemes

Provides accident insurance cover for death or disability at a very low premium.

Weather Based Crop Insurance Scheme

Insurance Schemes

Compensates farmers for crop loss caused by adverse weather conditions

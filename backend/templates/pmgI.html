Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

This scheme provides insurance cover for crops against natural disasters, pests, and diseases. Eligible farmers can claim compensation at low premium rates to recover from unexpected losses.

  • Protects crops from natural disasters
  • Low premium rates
  • Financial stability for farmers

Life insurance scheme that provides coverage of ₹2 lakh for farmers in case of death. Helps secure family income.

Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana

PMSBY is an accident insurance scheme launched by the Government of India.
It provides affordable personal accident insurance coverage to individuals in case of accidental death or permanent disability.
The scheme is mainly aimed at people aged 18–70 years who have a bank account.

  • Life coverage of ₹2 lakh
  • ₹1 lakh for permanent partial disability
  • Financial security for farmer's family

Government Scheme Details

Why this scheme is important

Farmers face financial losses due to floods, droughts, pests and other natural calamities. This scheme provides financial support and security to farmers during such losses.

Eligibility Criteria

  • All farmers including small and marginal farmers
  • Both loanee and non-loanee farmers
  • Farmers growing notified crops

Benefits of the Scheme

  • Financial compensation for crop loss
  • Low insurance premium rates
  • Coverage for natural calamities

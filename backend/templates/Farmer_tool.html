Farming Tools & Equipment Support

Government schemes provide modern tools and machinery to improve farm efficiency and reduce labor cost.
These schemes promote mechanization and technology adoption in agriculture.
Farmers get access to advanced equipment at subsidized rates.

Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization

Farm Equipment Scheme

Provides subsidy on tractors, harvesters, and modern farming tools.

Custom Hiring Centers (CHC)

Farm Equipment Scheme

Allows farmers to rent agricultural machinery at affordable rates.

PM KUSUM Yojana

Farm Equipment Scheme

Supports installation of solar pumps and renewable energy equipment.

Soil Health Card Scheme

Farm Support Scheme

Provides soil testing and nutrient recommendations for better yield.

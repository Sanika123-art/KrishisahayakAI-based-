Loans provided by Government

Government loan schemes help farmers meet agricultural expenses such as seeds, fertilizers, equipment, and irrigation.
These schemes offer low-interest loans and easy repayment options to reduce financial burden.
They promote sustainable farming and improve farmers’ income and productivity.

Kisan Credit Card(Scheme)

Loan Scheme

Provides short-term credit for crop production and farming needs at low interest rates.

Agriculture Infrastructure Fund

Loan Scheme

Offers loans for building farm infrastructure like storage, cold chains, and warehouses.

PM MUDRA Yojana (For Agri Allied)

Loan Scheme

Provides financial support to farmers for agri-business and allied activities.

NABARD Refinance Scheme

Loan Scheme

Supports farmers with long-term credit for agriculture and rural development.
